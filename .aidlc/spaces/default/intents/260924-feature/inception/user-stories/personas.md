# Personas

## Customer

- **Name**: Alex
- **Role**: Online shopper
- **Goals**: Find the right pair of shoes quickly, compare styles and prices, complete purchase easily, track orders
- **Pain points**: Overwhelming choice, unclear sizing, complicated checkout, slow delivery
- **Context**: Shops on weekends, uses mobile and desktop, prefers free shipping and easy returns
- **Priority ranking**: High - core shopping functionality

## Admin

- **Name**: Sam
- **Role**: Store manager
- **Goals**: Manage inventory, view sales reports, process orders, update product information
- **Pain points**: Inventory inaccuracies, slow order processing, difficult product updates
- **Context**: Works during business hours, uses desktop interface, manages multiple shoe categories
- **Priority ranking**: High - inventory and order management

## Guest

- **Name**: Taylor
- **Role**: Casual browser
- **Goals**: Browse products without commitment, quick checkout as guest
- **Pain points**: Forced account creation, limited guest options, difficulty finding saved cart
- **Context**: Shops occasionally, uses mobile primarily, wants fast checkout
- **Priority ranking**: Medium - convenient guest experience
